# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

FileTopo is an open project whose subject — people's folders and documents —
touches their private lives. We therefore expect particular care from everyone
who takes part, both in respecting other people and in respecting their data.

We commit to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race,
religion, sexual orientation, or language.

## Expected behaviour

- Showing empathy, patience and kindness.
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive criticism.
- Owning our mistakes, apologising to those affected, and learning from them.
- Putting the interests of the project and its users first.
- Writing in English or French, as you prefer, without demanding either
  language of the person you are talking to.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome advances.
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, or personal and political attacks
  directed at a person.
- Harassment, public or private.
- Publishing other people's private information — postal or email address,
  identity, file paths, screenshots of personal documents — without their
  explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## A rule specific to this project: other people's data

This project analyses file trees. As a consequence:

- **Never** attach the contents, names or paths of real documents belonging to
  anyone — including yourself — to an issue, a contribution, a message or a
  screenshot.
- Use synthetic data only, as described in
  [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and
  `tests/fixtures_synthetic/README.md`.
- A contribution or report containing real personal data will be edited or
  declined. That is not a judgement of the person who submitted it.

This rule protects third parties and contributors alike.

## Scope

This code applies in every project space — repository, issues, pull requests,
discussions — and whenever someone represents the project in a public space.

## Enforcement

The project is currently maintained by one person. Reports can be sent to the
maintainer through the GitHub profile linked in [README.md](README.md).

There is no dedicated reporting address today. That limit is acknowledged and
documented; it will be revisited if the project takes on more people.

The maintainer commits to reviewing every report, to respecting the privacy and
safety of the reporter, and to responding proportionately: a warning, removal
of content, declining a contribution, or, as a last resort, exclusion from the
project's spaces.

Security vulnerabilities are not covered by this document: see
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
